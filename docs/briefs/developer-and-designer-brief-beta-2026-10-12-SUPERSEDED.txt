VOICES — Developer and Designer Brief
  ​Oct 12, 2026       · ​@Toox Hub

Voices is a voice-first social app: you post by speaking, others listen, react with emojis and
reply by typing. This brief gives your developer and designer everything needed to launch
by 26 October 2026.


What Is New in Version 2
     Open platform: everyone can see everyone. Every profile and every public Voice is
     visible and searchable by any user. No private accounts, no follower-only Voices.
     Category alerts: users choose categories and are notified when a new Voice appears
     in them, with throttles so alerts stay fun and don't become noise.
     Gossip club feel: live counters, a Hot Right Now strip, heating-up alerts and scheduled
     Tea Time digests, so there are no dull moments.
     Unchanged: anonymous posting, paid exclusive Voices, Voice Credits, brand adverts,
     and a protected Voices Care.

Full specs are in sections 3.17 and 3.18.



1. What We Are Building
Voices starts in Nigeria and then goes across Africa. Mental health is one protected area
(Voices Care). Creators and brands can sell through WHO MADE THIS (WMT). Launch
date: 26 October 2026, Android first.

The non-negotiables

 1. Every post is a real human voice, recorded live inside the app. No uploads, no AI voices.
 2. Comments are typed. Reactions are emojis.
 3. A profile is a profile image, a handle and a voice bio (the Voice Card). There is no typed
    bio.
 4. Posts are Named or Anonymous. Anonymous means a masked voice, a random alias,
    and no trace on the profile.
 5. Posting costs Voice Credits, like airtime. Listening, reacting and commenting are free.
 6. Up to 3 hashtags per post.
 7. Brands pay for labelled Sponsored Voices, recorded by a real human.
 8. Voices Care is always free, with no ads, no credits, and its own feed.

2. Design Brief
Mood. Fun, bold, sound-first and Nigerian in spirit. Think a lively market, not a hospital
waiting room. Use the WAWU purple as an anchor with energetic accent colours. Voices
Care switches to a calmer, softer theme.

Principles

     Sound is the star. Waveforms are the main visual element, and they move when audio
     plays.
     One thumb. Every main action reachable with one hand. Big record button.
     Celebrate, don't guilt. Confetti on a first post, friendly sounds on reactions, and no
     guilt-trip streaks or "we miss you" nudges.
     Light and fast. Works on low-end Android phones and weak networks. Small
     animations, no heavy video.
     Dark and light mode, large text support, and screen-reader labels on every control.

Fun details to design

     A record button that squishes, pulses and shows a live waveform and countdown ring.
     Avatars that pulse and glow while their Voice plays.
     An emoji reaction tray that pops up with a small bounce and haptic tap.
     Voice cards that unfold with a satisfying motion when you open them.
     A wallet with a recharge screen that feels like topping up airtime: quick amounts, one
     tap.
     Playful empty states and error messages in a friendly Nigerian voice.
     Subtle sound design: a short chime for posting, a soft pop for reactions, all with an off
     switch.

3. Functional Specification

3.1 Accounts and sign-up
     One WAWU Identity account, shared with WHO MADE THIS. Sign up with phone
     number (OTP) or email.
     Steps: phone or email, handle, profile image, voice check (read a random phrase aloud),
     record the voice bio, choose interests.
     18+ only. Date of birth is collected and stored as an age check, not displayed.
     At launch, a human reviewer approves each new account after the voice check.
     Invite codes are optional and can be used to pace sign-ups.


3.2 Profile and Voice Card
     Profile image: upload or take a photo, cropped to a circle. Images are moderated
     automatically and by humans. Anonymous posts never show it.
     Handle: unique, 3 to 20 characters, filtered for banned words.
     Voice bio: 30 to 60 seconds, recorded in-app. Plays from a single tap on any profile or
     on any speaker's name. No typed bio.
     Transcript: auto-generated for accessibility, shown on request.
     Verified Human mark (after the voice check) and WMT Verified mark (from WMT).
     Sell on WMT link: a button that opens the person's WMT profile. Shown only if the
     account is linked to WMT.
     Followers, following, pinned Voice, Voices grid. Anonymous Voices never appear here.


3.3 Record and post
     In-app recording only. No file upload, no import, no editing that inserts outside audio.
     Trim start and end only.
     Length: up to 3 minutes (configurable). Voices Care: up to 10 minutes.
     Before publishing: choose a category (required), up to 3 hashtags (typed or
     suggested), Anonymous or Named, and the Explicit language toggle.
     The screen shows the credit cost before the user taps Publish.
     Preview and re-record are free until published.
     After publishing: the Voice goes to review (at launch, every post is reviewed by a
     person before going live).
     Drafts are saved on the device for 7 days.

3.4 Feed and discovery
     Tabs: Following (newest first), Discover (editor picks plus ranking by listen-through
     and reactions like Felt this and Thank you), Nearby (city or region), Trending (by
     hashtag and category).
     New Voices go to a small audience first and spread only after passing checks.
     Clean Mode on by default hides Voices labelled Explicit language.
     In Voices Care, no infinite scroll without a pause: show a That's enough for now card
     after a set number of Voices.


3.5 Player
     Streaming audio with speed control (0.75x to 2x), 15-second skip, sleep timer, and
     background play.
     Offline saves for saved Voices.
     No download button, and share links open the in-app or web player.
     Content-warning gate on flagged Voices.


3.6 Comments
     Typed, threaded one level deep. Text only. No emojis, images, GIFs or links.
     Only voice-verified accounts can comment. Rate limits apply.
     Comments are open to every verified account. Speakers can filter keywords, hold
     flagged comments for approval, and block or mute users.
     Automatic hiding of slurs, threats, phone numbers and spam, with human review.
     Report and block on every comment.

3.9 Categories
Government, Family, School, Career, Relationship, Sex (18+, education and honest talk
only), Gospel and Faith, Confessions, Hot Takes, Storytime, Money and Hustle, Japa and
Abroad, Workplace Gist, Rants, Marriage and Dating, Comedy, Spoken Word and

Motivation, Market, Maker Stories, and Mental Health (Voices Care). Categories are
config, not code, so they can change without an app update.


3.10 Anonymous posting
     Toggle on the Record screen.
     The voice is always masked (signal processing only, never AI conversion), with a
     different mask on every post.
     Shown as Anonymous with a random alias for that Voice. The same alias is used for the
     poster's comments on it.
     Never on the profile, never in the Following feed, no follower notifications.
     Shows "today" instead of an exact time.
     The raw voice is checked for human voice and then deleted. Only the masked audio
     and the check result are kept.
     Internally the post is linked to the account for safety and legal reasons, with strict
     access and audit logs.
     Anonymous accounts cannot attach WMT listings or sell.

3.12 Direct Voice and Gifts
     Direct Voice: a private Voice sent to someone with an open inbox, for a credit price set
     by the recipient within a band. Refunded if unanswered in 7 days. Recipients choose
     open, followers only or closed.
     Gifts: credits attached to a Voice reply, shown as a gift (named or anonymous).
     Creators can cash out earned credits in naira after identity checks, with a minimum
     payout and a platform fee.

3.12a Paid listening: exclusive Voices and updates
Listening to exclusive Voices and updates is a paid service. Everything else stays free to
listen to.

     Exclusive Voices: a creator marks a Voice as Exclusive and sets a price in credits.
     Listeners see a locked card with the title, duration, category, price and a free 10 to 15
     second preview.
     Updates: a subscriber-only feed of short Voices from a creator, such as news,
     announcements, tips or behind-the-scenes. Subscribers get a push notification for
     each one.
     Ways to pay: unlock a single Voice with credits, or subscribe to a creator monthly in
     credits for all their exclusives and updates. Creators choose which they offer.
     Pricing: set by the creator within bands held in the admin config. The platform fee is
     also config (placeholder: 30 percent).
     Access: an unlocked Voice stays playable for that account inside the app. No
     downloads, and the audio is watermarked to the listener's account to discourage
     sharing.
     Refunds: automatic if the audio fails or moderation removes it. Reports of a Voice not
     matching its description are reviewed within a set window. No refunds once most of a
     Voice has been listened to, except for faults.
     Moderation: Exclusive Voices follow every rule and are reviewed before they go on
     sale. Only named, verified creators can sell.
     Creator tools: price setting, a subscriber list, unlock and revenue stats, and cash-out
     of earned credits in naira after identity checks.
     Care: no paid listening for survivors' stories. Only verified professionals can offer paid
     content, as set out in the strategy document.
     Acceptance: a locked Voice shows its preview, unlocks after payment, and appears in
     the buyer's Unlocked list.

3.15 Notifications and search
     Category alerts are the core notification. Users pick categories at sign-up, with a
     permission prompt and a clear off switch. See 3.18 for the full rules.
     Types: new comment, new follower, reaction milestones, gifts, Direct Voice, followed
     hashtag trends, moderation decisions.
     Search: handles, hashtags, categories and transcripts.


3.16 Voices Care
     A separate tab and theme, with a separate feed and moderation queue.
     Always free. No ads, credits, gifts, promotion, selling, or public trending.
     Pinned crisis resources on every Care screen, and the crisis protocol described in the
     strategy document.
     Care opens only when the protocol and NGO partners are ready.


3.17 Open social graph (everyone can see everyone)
     Every profile and every public Voice is visible and searchable by every user. There are
     no private accounts and no follower-only Voices. Following is instant, with no approval
     step.
     Anyone can follow anyone, react to any Voice, and, if voice-verified, comment on any
     Voice.
     Search finds people by handle, Voices by title, hashtag or transcript, and categories.
     Shared Voice links play in a web player for anyone, with a prompt to open the app.
     Anonymous Voices are visible to everyone with the author hidden. Exclusive Voices
     show as locked cards. Direct Voices stay private.
     Suggested people to follow come from categories and activity. No phone-contact
     upload at launch.
     Safety controls stay: block (hides the blocker from the blocked person), mute, report,
     keyword filters, rate limits on new accounts, and 18+ only.
     Voices Care is outside the open graph. Care posts keep their own visibility settings and
     never appear in category alerts, trending or search.


3.18 Category alerts and the gossip club engine
Goal: something is always happening. When a Voice goes live in a category, the people
who chose that category hear about it straight away.

 1. Choose categories. At sign-up users pick at least 3 categories, and can change them
     any time in Settings.
 2. Alert level per category: Instant, Hourly digest, Daily digest or Off. Default is Instant.
 3. The trigger. When a Voice is approved and published, every subscriber with Instant
     alerts gets a push such as New in Confessions: Anonymous, 0:48, title. Tapping opens
     the player.
 4. Speed target. Most pushes go out within 60 seconds of approval (confirm with load
     tests).
 5. Fan-out design. A backend queue sends in batches to the push provider, reads each
     user's preferences at send time, is idempotent, and logs delivery. Use per-user throttle
     counters, not only broadcast topics.
 6. Anti-fatigue rules (all in admin config):
          Quiet hours default to 10 pm to 7 am local time, editable.
          Maximum 10 category pushes per user per hour. Extra Voices roll into one summary
          push, such as 9 new Voices in Hot Takes.
          One notification per category in the tray, replaced as new ones arrive.
          A speaker's posts trigger alerts at most a set number of times per hour.
          Voices from new or low-trust accounts do not trigger alerts until the account has a
          set number of approved posts.
 7. Followed people always get through ahead of category alerts.
 8. Live feel inside the app:
          Category chips show a pulsing badge with the number of new Voices since the last
          visit.
          A New Voices pill at the top of the feed loads them instantly.
          Each Voice shows a Listening now count (aggregate numbers only, never names).
          Reaction counts animate live.
          A Hot Right Now strip shows the top 5 Voices by listeners in the last 15 minutes.
 9. Heating up alerts. When a Voice passes a velocity threshold (listeners and listen-
     through in a short window), send one Heating up in Hot Takes alert to that category's
     subscribers.
10. Rituals. Scheduled alerts for #ConfessionWednesday, the Daily Question, Voice
     Battles, and Tea Time digests at 12 pm and 8 pm with the top Voices of the day.
11. Speaker alerts: reactions, comments, milestones (10, 100, 1,000 listens), gifts and
     unlocks.
12. Lock-screen privacy. A setting controls whether titles show. Default is on for general
     categories. Sex, Confessions and Workplace Gist always show generic text, such as
     New Voice in Confessions.

13. Care never sends category alerts.

Admin controls: a global pause switch, a per-category kill switch for events mode, per-
category caps, and alert analytics (sent, delivered, opened, opt-outs, mutes). If opt-outs
spike, the system backs off automatically. Alerts stop at once for any Voice that is
reported or flagged, and the circuit breaker applies.

Gossip guardrails (build these into the launch):

     Transcripts are scanned for names. A Voice that names a private individual is held for
     review and does not trigger alerts until cleared.
     A This is about me report goes to a priority queue with fast takedown.
     Criticism of public figures' public conduct is allowed. False factual claims are removed.
     No phone numbers, addresses or identity details of anyone.
     A burst of reports pauses the Voice's spread and its alerts.

Acceptance criteria

 1. A newly approved Voice sends a push to Instant subscribers of its category, within the
     speed target.
 2. After 10 pushes in an hour, extra Voices arrive as one summary push.
 3. Quiet hours hold pushes until morning.
 4. Setting a category to Off stops its alerts.
 5. A Voice crossing the heating-up threshold sends one alert only.
 6. Confessions alerts show generic text on the lock screen.
 7. Voices Care posts never trigger a category alert.
 8. A reported Voice stops sending alerts immediately.



4. Voice Integrity and Safety
     In-app capture only, with a secure recorder that blocks file injection.
     Liveness: a random phrase at sign-up and at random later checks.
     Voiceprint: a private template stored securely, used to match named accounts and
     stop duplicates and repeat abusers. Store templates, not raw recordings.
     Anti-spoofing: detect synthetic speech, replayed audio and splicing, run before
     masking. Test several providers and models.
     Masking: pitch and formant shifting only.
     Watermarking of served audio, no downloads, rate limits and bot detection to resist
     scraping and voice cloning.

VOICES — Developer and Designer Brief (Beta, 26 Oct 2026)




     Transcription and screening: every Voice and comment is transcribed and scanned for
     slurs, threats, personal details, scams and spam, with local-language word lists.
     Human review: at launch, every post is reviewed by a person first.
     Circuit breaker: a burst of reports pauses a Voice's spread.
     Penalties: warning, posting pause, permanent ban of account and voiceprint.
     Age: 18+ only.



5. Admin and Moderation Dashboard
     Review queue (pending posts, reports, flagged comments), with audio player,
     transcript and one-click actions: approve, reject with reason, warn, pause, ban.
     Account review for new sign-ups after the voice check.
     Strike and penalty history, appeals queue, and second-moderator review.
     Trending and hashtag review, with a block list.
     Ad and brand review and scheduling.
     Config screen: category list, credit prices, free allowance, length limits, reaction sets.
     Wallet admin: credit adjustments, refunds, voucher generation, fraud flags.
     Analytics: daily active users, posts, listens, listen-through, reports, response times,
     credit purchases, ad plays.
     Roles and audit logs on every sensitive action. Moderators see only what they need.

7. Technical Requirements
     Clients: Android first. Use a cross-platform framework so iOS follows quickly. Web
     listener for shared links.
     Audio: compressed streaming (such as AAC or Opus), adaptive for weak networks,
     CDN delivery, cold storage for old audio.

     Backend: API, queue-based processing for transcription, screening and anti-spoofing,
     and a search index.
     Performance: the app must be usable on low-end Android phones and on slow
     connections. Start playback quickly and cache the next Voice.
     Security: encryption in transit and at rest, separate keys for voiceprints, least-privilege
     access, audit logs, regular security testing.
     Privacy and legal: compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, treating
     voiceprints as sensitive biometric data, with consent screens, data export and
     deletion. Counsel signs off before launch.
     Analytics: event tracking for sign-up, record start, publish, play, listen-through, react,
     comment, purchase and report. No third-party ad trackers.
     Scalability: design for growth, with load testing before the public launch.

10. Acceptance Criteria (key flows)
 1. Sign-up: a new user can sign up, pass the live-phrase check, record a voice bio, and
     reach the feed once approved.
 2. Post: a user can record, choose a category and up to 3 hashtags, see the credit cost,
     publish, and see the Voice in review. Credits are deducted at publish.
 3. Anonymous: an anonymous post uses a masked voice and a random alias, and does not
     appear on the profile or Following feed.
 4. Listen: a Voice plays within 3 seconds on a normal connection, with speed control and
     a transcript toggle.
 5. React and comment: a verified user can react with one emoji and post a typed
     comment. Reports reach the moderation queue.
 6. Wallet: a user can recharge, see the balance update, and see a ledger entry for every
     credit movement. The free daily allowance resets daily.
 7. Profile: tapping Play on a profile plays the voice bio. A linked user sees a working Sell
     on WMT button.
