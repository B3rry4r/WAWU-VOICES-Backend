# VOICES Nigeria launch: what the owner's team asked for (7 Nov 2026)

Source: messages from Mr Lennox (owner's team, 6 Nov 2026, 00:39 to 01:22) passed on by the owner on 7 Nov 2026, the
"Viral Participation Engine" spec (pictures in `source/spec-*.jpg`), the pricing PDF (`source/VOICES_Pricing_Structure.pdf`,
see `PRICING.md`) and the landing picture (`source/landing-now-copy-to-replace.jpg`).
This file records what was asked, in their words where possible. The tasks in `/tasks` build it. Open questions are
marked OPEN and listed in `tasks/README.md`; a task that depends on one waits for the owner's answer.

## 1. Landing words (before "Listen as a guest")
Replace the current headline "Nigeria is talking. Add your voice." and the line "Every day our editors ask one
question…" with:
- Eyebrow: NIGERIA’S NATIONAL CONVERSATION
- Headline: Finally, a place to say it. And be heard.
- Intro line: One question a day about life in Nigeria. Say your answer in 10 seconds, or type it. Hear people who agree
  and people who don’t. Vote. Then back the ideas that could actually help.

## 2. The launch question ("on the 15th")
- Question: How do you feel about Nigeria right now?
- Mood words: Angry. Tired. Proud. Hopeful.
- Line: With everything going on, say it in your voice. 10 seconds.
- People pick ONE mood word, then record up to 10 seconds (or type). The mood word is saved as structured data on the
  post. They can change the word before publishing. The mood word shows beside each published response.
- OPEN Q1: the launch date. The site was planned for Thu 12 Nov; the launch question is "on the 15th" (Sun 15 Nov).

## 3. The broadcast
- Broadcast, SMS and emails to at least 1 million people, text:
  "Dear {{first_name}}, with all that is happening, how do you feel about Nigeria: angry, tired, proud or hopeful? Say it
  in 10 seconds: usevoices.com/go"
- OPEN Q2: the address. Everything is built for voices.wawuafrica.com; the message uses usevoices.com/go.
- A 1-million-person send can bring a very large burst of visits in minutes (task LCH-09).

## 4. Personal share card
Straight after posting, make a share card:
- "MY VOICE. MY NIGERIA." / "I feel HOPEFUL about Nigeria." / "What about you?" / a VOICES "Add your voice" button.
- The person’s name only if they agree. The VOICES logo and the campaign link.
- WhatsApp is the first way to share. The card is easy to save and forward.
- The line from section 5 (percentage or "You’re one of the first.") is on the card.

## 5. Show where people sit
- "You’re with the 38% who feel HOPEFUL." with a Nigeria / World switch.
- No percentage until at least 50 valid responses exist in that group. Before that: "You’re one of the first."
- Percentages come from unique, valid responses (one per person).

## 6. Share is the next step
- On the "You’re live" screen, Share is the main button. Order: WhatsApp, then SMS, copy link, the phone’s share sheet.
- Pre-filled message (one tap):
  "I just shared how I feel about Nigeria on VOICES.
  I feel [MOOD] about Nigeria. What about you?
  Add your voice in 10 seconds: [PERSONAL LINK]"
- SMS opens the person’s own messaging app. Never upload or read their contacts. Never send anything automatically.
  Sharing always needs the person’s own tap.

## 7. Ask for a person
- The message says "Send it to 3 people." Each person gets a personal invite link.
- The sender sees "2 of your friends have answered" and can tap to see each friend’s word.
- A friend’s word is shown to the person who invited them only if the friend leaves "Share my word" on (on by default).

## 8. In-person prompt
- The share screen says "Ask the person next to you, then compare" and shows a QR code of the invite link.

## 9. Share the voice as a video
- Turn the 10-second voice into a short video, 9:16 and 1:1: a moving waveform, captions, the question and the logo. No
  photos. At most 15 seconds, built in the background, only when the person asks. If it is not ready, share the audio link.

## 10. The weekly report is our Instagram post
- The weekly report is published every week for people to read what happened across the topics, and it becomes the
  team’s Instagram post each week.

## 11. Paid AI text fix
- "AI to adjust text or typo, people can pay for it. Only for that." The AI only corrects a person’s own text (spelling,
  typos); it is paid, and it is used for nothing else.
- OPEN Q6: the price of one fix.
