# Verify V-FIX-B, API side

Code under test: `v/fix-b` at f3eeb56 (origin/main f336fc6 is an ancestor, so nothing newer is missing). Checked on my own Postgres 16 (port 15432) and Redis (16379), both stopped now. No live provider was called; the mail relay and `fetch` were watched and stayed at zero. My attack specs are not committed (they live in the scratchpad, `scratchpad/specs/`). Only this report is committed.

Lead ruling respected: `isNews: false` and a post with no news tag are accepted and are not reported.

Summary: the five items are built and the core guarantees hold (no member can get Verified News by any field or route, the alert filters and idempotence hold, the migration is additive and reversible, the contract is byte-identical, all suites are green). One real hole in the Verified News guarantee (edit after approval) and four MEDIUM problems around the "nothing from a question that has not opened", the size behaviour, the unswitchable Verified notices, and the migration/calendar order tonight. Those are why this is FIX and not MERGE.

## Findings

### HIGH-1. A poster can rewrite a post after an editor has Verified it (proved by run)
- `api/src/talk/posts.service.ts:373` `editAnswer`. It checks only author, TEXT kind and the 5-minute window counted from `createdAt`. It does not look at `newsTag` or `newsReviewStatus`.
- Run: a member posts as Unverified News with `requestVerified` and source "Punch" (body "long original claim about fuel prices"); an editor approves (200, tag becomes VERIFIED_NEWS); the member then sends `PATCH /answers/{id}` `{"body":"fuel is free"}`. Result 200; row is `newsTag = VERIFIED_NEWS`, `body = "fuel is free"`. The request route and "Request Verified News at posting" are what make this reachable by an ordinary member: the editor checks one text, the member swaps in another, and it carries the Verified label, the source and the editors' name in the change log.
- Window: needs the approval to happen within 5 minutes of posting (the window counts from posting, not from approval). Editors working a live queue can do that. An editor's own Verified post is editable by that editor in the same way, which is the editor's own act.
- Should happen: an edit to a post that is Verified News (or has an open request) either is refused, or drops the tag back to Unverified News and reopens/clears the request, or is allowed only for EDITOR/ADMIN.
- Addendum acceptance: "a normal user cannot mark a post Verified News". The tag stays, but the content under it is no longer what was verified.

### MEDIUM-1. TAG_FOLLOW goes out for a post whose question is not open, and its text is the question's text (proved by run)
- `api/src/reach/alerts.service.ts:234` `tagPost` checks the answer (PUBLISHED, news, tag, not deleted) and never the question's window. `news.service.ts` (admin) `:401` and `posts.service.ts:324` call it.
- Run: a post made while its question was open, then the question set back to SCHEDULED with `startsAt` tomorrow (this is what the override rule "the replaced question moves to the next slot" does to a question already open). Then: request-verified answered 201, APPROVE_VERIFIED 200, `GET /news?tag=VERIFIED_NEWS` listed the post, `GET /news/{id}` 200, and `tagPost` wrote a TAG_FOLLOW notice to a follower whose body is the unopened question's text. Hard rule for tonight: "nothing from a question that has not opened may be shown or announced". The Newsroom listing (`news.service.ts` `visible`) is the older gap; the new announce path is V-FIX-B's.
- No normal member can create such a post before launch (posting on a future, draft or closed-draft question gave 404 in my run), so no leak by ordinary use tonight. It needs a question moved back after it opened.
- Should happen: `tagPost` (and request/approve) skip when the question is not inside its window (`isQuestionOpen`), the same helper everything else uses.
- Related, same cause, proved: an editor's `CHANGE_TAG` on a 3-week-old post on a closed question sent every follower of the new tag "New under Unverified News" with the old question's text. A correction to an old post is announced as new.
- Poll side (smaller): a poll row that has ended whose question has not opened sent POLL_CLOSED to a person who saved that question (1 notice, contrived data; `pollClosed` does not check the question window). Matching fix.

### MEDIUM-2. Nobody can switch Verified News notices off (read + proved by run)
- `api/src/me/me.service.ts:775` `setTagFollows` always keeps VERIFIED_NEWS; `reach.service.ts:40` `SETTING_FOR_TYPE` has no switch for TAG_FOLLOW ("the rest always arrive"). Every new account follows Verified. So the "switch" for this notice is not there.
- What a person receives on a day with 30 Verified posts (run, clock fixed): 30 posts inside one clock hour gives 1 notice; 30 posts spread over 8 clock hours gives 8 (9 in my run with one earlier hour); the most is one per clock hour, 24 a day, for Verified alone, plus up to 24 each for Gossip and Unverified for people who follow them. The cap is the clock hour, not a rolling hour: posts at 10:59:59 and 11:00:01 gave 2 notices two seconds apart. The notice points at the first post of the hour; later posts in that hour are never announced. Approvals and tag changes also count as "new posts" (see MEDIUM-1).
- Should happen: owner decision (the builder raised it as owner question 3). Smallest safe fix: a "News tags" switch in settings (a `notifyTagFollows`-style flag) honoured in `fanOut`, or a daily cap lower than 24.

### MEDIUM-3. Every tagged post scans the whole follower list, even when nothing can be written (proved by run)
- `alerts.service.ts:256-286`, `fanOut`. The hour key stops duplicates but not the work. 50,000 followers of Verified (45,000 ACTIVE, 5,000 suspended):
  - first Verified post: 45,001 notices written in 10.5 s, 303 INSERT statements, slowest INSERT 15.6 ms, slowest statement of any kind 15.6 ms (so the longest lock held is about 16 ms, each page its own implicit transaction, no long transaction). Process RSS 361 MB to a peak of 505 MB (heap peak 283 MB, includes the test harness); worst event-loop stall 108 ms.
  - a person posting at that moment: an ordinary post took 42-95 ms with nothing running and 123-252 ms (2 to 4 times) while the fan-out ran; all returned 201. (One post of the six in the burst got 402, free allowance spent, unrelated.)
  - a second run on the same post: wrote 0, took 9.1 s. A second Verified post in the same hour: wrote 0, took 9.4 s. Ten tagged posts at once: wrote 0, took 78.5 s of 20-connection pool contention. Nothing limits how many run together, and they run in the API process (`tagPostSafe` is fire-and-forget).
- Verified is posted only by editors (about 30 a day is safe: 30 x 9 s, one at a time). Gossip and Unverified are open to every member but have only opt-in followers. So no tonight problem; it becomes one if a tag gets tens of thousands of followers and members post under it.
- Should happen: one cheap "already announced this hour" check (a marker row or Redis key per tag and hour) before paging, and one fan-out per tag at a time.

### MEDIUM-4. Migration backfill and the launch calendar shift: order (read only, conditional)
- `20261114160000_fixb_alerts/migration.sql:6-7` marks every Poll with `endsAt <= now` and every Question with `startsAt <= now` as already announced. `infra/deploy/deploy.sh:18-29` runs `prisma migrate deploy` first and then, only if `.calendar-launch-shift-done` does not exist, `calendar:launch-shift`, which moves seeded SCHEDULED questions and polls forward (week 1 from 12 Nov to 16 Nov). If this deploy is the one that first applies the shift, the shifted questions and question polls were marked "alerted" before they were moved, and `sweep` skips them: no CATEGORY_ALERT or POLL_CLOSED for those. A miss of the feature, no leak. If the shift already ran on the droplet (it shipped in #12), no effect. I cannot see the droplet's marker.
- Should happen: check the marker on the droplet before deploy; if absent, either run the shift first or set the two columns back to NULL for rows with `status = 'SCHEDULED'` after it.

### LOW
1. **LISTEN counted for an advert outside its dates** (proved). `ads.ts:173` `record` gates on `status = APPROVED` only. Paused, rejected and awaiting adverts answered 404 and counted 0 (good). Adverts dated in the future or the past answered 204 and counted 1 each. Same gap exists for IMPRESSION and TAP on main; the new LISTEN inherits it.
2. **A guest can inflate listens with fresh keys** (proved). 50 different random `X-Guest-Id` UUIDs gave 50 listens, all 204. Same key repeated counts once (proved, also under 5 parallel). Garbage keys (`abc`, an SQL string, 10,000 characters, a space) gave 422 and a blank header 204 uncounted. A signed-in person who sends 5 different guest keys still counted once (proved). Bound: the route is throttled to 300 a minute per caller (read, `ads.ts:230`). The day is the Lagos day, UTC+1 (`lagosDay`, proved: 22:59Z is 14 Nov, 23:30Z is 15 Nov), so "per day" turns over at 23:00 UTC, the same minute as the launch.
3. **Advert accepts a removed clip, and a removed clip still plays** (proved). `ads.service.ts:393-403` checks READY, purpose and length but not `removedAt` (the override check does). A READY clip with `removedAt` set was accepted (201), approved (200), the placement then gave an AUDIO_SLOT ad with `audioClipId: null` (an audio advert with nothing to play), and `GET /voice-clips/{id}/play` answered 200 to a guest.
4. **Owner of the clip is not checked** (proved). An ad manager can attach another ad manager's AD_AUDIO clip; an editor another editor's or an admin's OVERRIDE_NOTE; and a clip with those purposes that a plain member made (possible before this release, the role rule is new) was accepted by both an advert and an override, which would make that member's recording public. Also an override accepted another person's OVERRIDE_IMAGE picture. Only reachable through clips made before the new role rule.
5. **Public after the ad ends** (proved). An approved advert's clip played for a guest (200) while the advert was future-dated, and after its end date passed, because `clips.service.ts:391` only asks for `status = APPROVED`. By id only; nothing lists it. An override's note stays public after it ends (`ENDED`, by the builder's design, read) and is 404 while scheduled and after cancel (proved).
6. **Override link is stored as sent** (proved). Only `https://` is accepted (`overrides.service.ts:266-275`: `http://`, `javascript:`, `data:`, `vbscript:`, `//x`, `file:`, spaces, NUL all gave 422, and over 500 characters gave 422). But the accepted string is saved raw: `https://x.com/"onmouseover="alert(1)`, `https://ok.com/\njavascript:alert(1)` (with a real newline), `https://user:pw@x.com` and `https://ok.com\@evil.com` were stored. Nothing here runs script (all are https and a page escapes the attribute), but a newline or quote in a stored link is untidy and user-info links can mislead. The task text says "http and https"; the code is https only (stricter, same as before). Should store `new URL(x).href` and refuse user-info.
7. **A declined request can be asked again at once** (proved, 201). No limit on request, decline, request. Editors can be spammed one at a time.
8. **Contract text** (read): `docs/contract/src/schemas.py` CreateAnswer description reads "isNews requires newsTag VERIFIED_NEWS needs an editor": a lost full stop. And `rollback.down.sql:3` names the group key `category:<id>`; the code writes `category-alert:<id>` (comment only, the SQL is right).
9. **Placement beside a Politics-category question** (proved, not new). An untargeted audio advert is shown beside a question in a "Politics" category that is not flagged `sensitive`; an advert targeted at Politics cannot be approved (422, proved); a sensitive question or topic gets no advert (proved for the audio slot). The kept-away rule works on the question's flag and on targeting, as it always has, for every kind. If the brief means "never beside a politics category", that is an older gap, not this branch's.
10. **Null duration passes the 30 s rule** (read): `(c.durationMs ?? 0) > 30000`. A READY clip always has a duration set by processing (`clips.service.ts:313`), so I could not make it happen; if one ever has none, the limit is bypassed. 0 ms and 29.999 s are accepted.
