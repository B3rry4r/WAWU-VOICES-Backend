# VOICES: things only a real phone can prove (owner's test list before launch)
Source: docs/audio.md (12 items) + verifiers. Each: what to do, what should happen.
1. iPhone Safari: tap Record the first time. One microphone prompt. After Stop, the orange mic dot goes away.
2. iPhone Safari: while recording, the bars move with your voice (not flat).
3. iPhone: record in Low Power Mode or with the screen dimmed, stop near 10 s. It posts, never "could not process".
4. iPhone: the silent switch on. A voice still plays through the speaker or earphones.
5. Bluetooth earphones: record with the earphone mic; playback through them.
6. A call comes in while recording: recording stops cleanly; Record again works.
7. Lock the screen while recording: recording stops; the take is kept or cleanly discarded.
8. Weak network: reload during upload, then send again. One answer appears.
9. Guest: record, then sign in (real WAWU ID, after the WAWU ID paste). The recording is still there and posts once.
10. Android Chrome: the Back button during recording asks before discarding.
11. Android: the keyboard does not cover the Write box.
12. Buy units with a real card (smallest pack), then post. Balance and receipt right. (Owner, once on the droplet.)
13. Share a question to WhatsApp: the preview shows the VOICES card.
14. Sign up on the phone: the 6-digit email code fills from the keyboard suggestion (one-time code).
15. Profile photo: pick from the library and from the camera; the photo shows after posting.
16. Android Back on the sign-up sheets goes back one step, never out of the app's flow.
17. Record, then press Close: you land back on the question and the mic dot goes off.
18. Payment failed screen: tap the reference's copy button on Android and iPhone; the full reference pastes into WhatsApp.

37. Appeal sheet with the keyboard open, and the Android Back button while it is open.
38. Pick a profile photo on a weak network: a clear message if it fails.

40. Delete account and Undo on a phone: both buttons easy to reach and work.

42. Share a profile and a poll through the phone's share sheet and WhatsApp.
