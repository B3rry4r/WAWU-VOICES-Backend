Community rules

Last updated 16 Nov 2026

VOICES is for strong opinions and real disagreement. We step in for harm, never for a point of view.

  1. Speak your mind, disagree hardGo after the idea, not the person.
  2. No threats, harassment or hateThat includes attacks on someone's tribe, faith, gender or disability.
  3. Keep private details privateNo phone numbers, home addresses or account numbers, yours or anyone's.
  4. News carries a tagSharing news? Pick Unverified News or Gossip. Only WAWU editors mark Verified News.
  5. One person, one voteNobody can buy votes. We remove coordinated voting and scams.
  6. Adults onlyYou must be 18 or older to use VOICES.

When a post breaks a rule

  1. 1Someone reports it, or it reaches 500 people and a moderator checks it before it spreads further.
  2. 2A moderator listens, reads the transcript and decides. Every decision is logged with a reason.
  3. 3The post is removed, or the account is warned or suspended. You are told why.
  4. 4Think we got it wrong? Appeal from the notice. A different moderator looks again.
Use Report on the post and choose "This is about me". It goes to the front of the queue.

Always free

Reading, listening, liking and sharing never cost anything. Paid posts always show their price first.
