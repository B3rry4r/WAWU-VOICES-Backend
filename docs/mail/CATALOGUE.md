The owner's 83 templates were written without knowing how VOICES is built, so every trigger, word and link was checked against the code. Group A (account mail) is M-a's, except the three VOICES-side sends A10 to A12 (round 2, below). Source of each row: the owner's Voices_Email_Catalogue.xlsx. All of B to H are notices sent from the VOICES API through WAWU ID's mail relay (`POST /internal/mail/send`, service key, Idempotency-Key); sender and reply-to are WAWU Africa's existing setup (owner, 12 Nov). The templates live in `api/src/mail/templates/` (html and text), sent or not.
