## What we collect
- From your WAWU ID when you sign in: your WAWU ID number and your country. The sign-in also carries your email address and phone number, but we do not keep your email address in the VOICES database, and your phone number is kept only as described next.
- Your phone number is never stored. We keep only a one-way scrambled form of it (a hash). It cannot be turned back into your number. We use it so that one phone number can have one account and one vote.
- What you give us: your handle, the display name you choose, photo, short bio, languages, state and city if you add them, and the topics you follow.
- What you post: voice recordings, the written transcript of each recording, text posts and replies, votes, reactions, likes and saves.
- Reports you file and decisions we make on reports and appeals.
- Payments: what you bought, the amount, the date and the payment reference. Card payments are made on Flutterwave. We keep the last four digits of the card and the payment method, never the full card number.
- Device and session data: your browser type, a scrambled device fingerprint used only to stop people abusing the free posts, and when your device was last seen. Our servers also see your network address when you connect.
- Result sheet photos, if you add one to a result post.

## Why we use it
To run VOICES: sign you in, show your posts, count votes once per person, take payments, keep the community safe, answer reports, and write the weekly report. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

## Where it is stored
VOICES runs on DigitalOcean. The database is on our own DigitalOcean server. Voice recordings, photos and share-card images are kept in DigitalOcean Spaces, in a bucket used only by VOICES. Nightly database backups are kept for 7 days.

## Who else handles it
- Flutterwave handles payments. We send them what is needed to take a payment, and they tell us whether it worked.
- Google Gemini turns voice recordings into transcripts, and helps draft the weekly report from posts. For the weekly report, handles and phone numbers are removed from the text before it is sent, and the draft is told never to name or quote a person.
- WAWU ID, run by WAWU, signs you in.
- We may share information with the authorities where the law requires it.

## Anonymous posting
Where a post is marked anonymous, other people see no name, handle or photo, and the post is not linked to your profile. The VOICES team can still see who wrote an anonymous post, but only to deal with a report, a legal request or abuse. Nobody else can.

## Result sheet photos
A photo of a result sheet is never public. Only the WAWU team can see it, to check the numbers. The post itself shows totals only.

## How long we keep it
- Your account data and posts stay while your account is open. Posts you delete are removed from the app.
- Delete your account and we keep it for 14 days. Then your profile details, posts, replies, likes, reactions, saves, blocks, notifications, settings and devices are deleted. Your recordings are removed from the app at once and their files are deleted from storage by a regular clean-up, and transcripts are wiped. Unspent Voice Units are lost.
- Some records stay, with no name or phone hash attached: votes (so poll totals stay true), payment and receipt records (so the books add up), and moderation and audit records. [OWNER: confirm how long payment records must be kept under Nigerian law.]
- Database backups are kept for 7 days.

## Cookies and local storage
VOICES does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a small amount of data in your browser: your signed-in session, and your light or dark theme choice. It may also keep a draft you have not posted yet, and a payment you have started, so you can pick up where you left off. Clearing your browser data removes them.

## Your rights
Subject to the law, you can ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, object to some uses, or complain to the data protection authority. You can export your data and delete your account in Settings. For anything else, write to support@wawuafrica.com.

## Changes
If we change this notice, the date at the top changes too.
