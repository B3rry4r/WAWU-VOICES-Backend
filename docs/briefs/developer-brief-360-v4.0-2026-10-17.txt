1. What We Are Building
VOICES is Nigeria's national conversation app. Editors publish curated questions. People
answer in text or short voice messages, like and react, vote in polls, and share
conversations to WhatsApp, Instagram and other apps. People start free with 10 posts,
then buy Voice Units to keep posting. Brands pay for labelled advert placements.

Platforms: Android first. A fast mobile web page for every shared link. iOS and a web app
follow.

2. Product Rules (Non-Negotiable)
 1. Editors curate the questions. Users answer them.
 2. Answers are text or voice. Voice is 10 seconds by default, with 30-second and 60-
     second options through a Voice Pass.
 3. Reading, listening, liking and sharing are always free.
 4. Every account gets 10 free posts (text or audio). After that, posting uses Voice Units,
     from a minimum purchase of ₦138.

 5. All prices, units, passes and limits come from the admin configuration. Amounts are in
    whole naira, and units can be fractional so any price lands on a complete amount.
 6. Nobody can buy extra voting power.
 7. Paid placements are labelled and never mixed into organic ranking.
 8. Raw audio files can never be downloaded from the app.

4. Key User Flows
4.1 First session. Open a shared link or the app → the question and top answers load with
no sign-up wall → the user can listen, like and share as a guest → on the first post, react or
vote, the sign-up prompt appears → phone number and one-time code → handle and 3
interests → back to the question with the answer box ready.

4.2 The first 10 free posts. The user answers by text or voice. A counter shows 7 free
posts left. Text and audio both count. Deleting a post does not return the free count.

4.3 The paywall and buying units. On the 11th post the user sees You have used your 10
free posts. Get Voice Units from ₦138. A bottom sheet shows the tiers. The user picks a
tier, pays, sees the units arrive, and the post continues without losing what they wrote or
recorded.

4.4 Voice Pass. In the voice recorder the user sees Need more time? Voice 30 or Voice 60.
The pass price is shown in units and in naira. After purchase the recorder limit changes for
the pass period.

4.5 Sharing. The user taps share on any answer or question. A share sheet shows
WhatsApp, WhatsApp Status, Instagram Stories, Facebook, X, TikTok, Telegram, SMS,
Copy link and More. A share card is generated and sent with a link.

4.6 Voting. The user opens a poll, picks an option, confirms, and sees live results. One vote
per account, with the cost shown before confirming where a vote costs units.

4.7 Adverts. A user sees a labelled Sponsored placement. Tapping opens a brand page or
link. Plays, taps and listens are recorded for the advertiser's report.

6. Feature Specifications

6.1 Questions engine
     Question fields: text, category, start and end time, status (draft, scheduled, live,
     closed), featured flag, type (daily, major, flash, community), sponsor (optional).
     Cadence: a daily question, a major national conversation every three days that stays
     pinned, and flash polls for live events.
     Editors can schedule, close, feature and publish follow-up questions, and link a
     question to a poll.
     Closed questions stay readable but stop accepting answers.


6.2 Answers
Text: up to 500 characters (config). Replies are one level deep. A user can delete their
own post at any time and edit text within 5 minutes.

Voice:

 1. Tap the microphone and grant permission with a clear explainer.
 2. Record with a visible countdown. Auto-stop at the limit (10 seconds by default; 30 or 60
     with a Voice Pass).
 3. Preview with play, pause and progress.
 4. Choose Send, Re-record or Delete. Nothing publishes without Send.
 5. Record in the app only. No file upload.




                                                                                            Page 4 of 14
VOICES — Developer Brief 360 (v4.0)




 6. Encode to a compressed format (for example Opus or AAC at about 24 kbps, so 10
     seconds is about 30 KB), upload with retry, and show progress.
 7. Playback: play and pause, duration, progress, optional speed. No autoplay of the next
     answer.
 8. Transcribe each clip for moderation, search and captions (see 6.10).

Equal footing: text and voice have the same ranking and discovery.


6.3 Likes and reactions
                                                                      ❤️ Relatable
     Like is a one-tap heart (also double-tap). It is free and counts as the
     reaction.
     Reactions open from a long press or the reaction button: 🔥 Powerful, 💀 Wild, ❤️
     Relatable, 😂 Funny, 😮 Surprising, 😢 Sad, 👏 Well said, 🤔 Thought-provoking, 💯
     Exactly, 👎 Disagree.
     One reaction per user per answer, changeable or removable. Changing is never
     charged again.
     Free reactions per day: 20 (config). After that, a small unit charge (config, default 0.1
     unit).
     Likes and shares are always free.


6.4 Ranking and discovery
     Signals: unique participants, positive and negative engagement, meaningful replies,
     relevance, recency and engagement over time, quality signals, and a manipulation
     score.
     Fair chance: every new answer is shown to a first group of readers. Strong early
     engagement widens its reach. Follower count is not an input.
     Disagreement alone never buries an answer.
     Labels: Trending, Most Relatable, Most Thought-Provoking, Most Debated, Most
     Supported, Most Surprising.
     Paid placements are stored and ranked in a separate system, and always labelled.


6.5 Voting
     Poll types: open polls (one vote per account and phone number, labelled open) and
     verified-voter polls (later).
     Single and multiple choice, start and end time, live or periodic results, total valid votes
     and percentages, results linked to the question.
     Integrity: duplicate-vote prevention, device checks, rate limits, anomaly detection, and
     an append-only vote log that can be audited.

     Cost: 3 free votes a day, then a small unit charge (config, default 0.2 unit). Public-
     interest polls are always free. Nobody can buy extra votes.
     Cache results for hot polls to survive spikes.
     Never label an open poll a national survey.

Price build-up (illustration for finance, confirm tax and fees). A ₦138 Spark purchase:
gross ₦138.00, VAT at 7.5 percent (if applicable) about ₦9.63, payment fee about 1.5
percent or ₦2.07, leaving about ₦126.30 on the web. If the purchase goes through app-
store billing, the store takes a further share, usually 15 to 30 percent. Prices must be
adjustable so these costs can be recovered.

Ledger requirements

     Store every amount as an integer. Use 1 unit = 100 centi-units, and prices in kobo, so
     there are no floating-point errors.
     Double-entry ledger with one entry per credit and debit, linked to the purchase, post
     or pass that caused it.
     A balance can never go below zero. Spending is atomic with the action it pays for. If the
     post fails, the spend is reversed.
     Idempotent payment handling, with a unique key per purchase, so retries never
     double-credit.
     Payment webhooks verified, daily reconciliation with the payment partner, failed-
     payment recovery, and receipts.
     Refund rules and unit expiry are configuration (recommended: units do not expire in
     the first year).
     Fraud controls: velocity limits, device checks, chargeback and refund flags, and admin
     adjustment with a reason and an audit log.
     Units are not cashable and not transferable at launch.


6.7 Free allowance and paywall logic
     A lifetime counter free_posts_remaining starts at 10 for each verified phone number.
     Text and audio posts both use it. Deleting a post does not restore it.



                                                                                           Page 7 of 14
VOICES — Developer Brief 360 (v4.0)




     One account per phone number. Device and network checks limit abuse of the free
     allowance.
     On exhaustion, the next post triggers the buy-units sheet. The draft text or recording is
     kept so the user does not lose it.
     Optional (config): a small daily free post after the first 10, to protect the habit.
     Reading, listening, liking, sharing and the free reaction and vote allowances stay
     available even with a zero balance.


6.8 Sharing and social handoff
     Share targets: WhatsApp (chat and Status), Instagram (Stories and feed), Facebook, X,
     TikTok, Telegram, SMS, Copy link, and the system share sheet.
     Share card: generated on the server in two sizes, 1080 by 1920 (stories) and 1080 by
     1080 (square). A text answer becomes an image with the question, the answer and the
     VOICES brand. A voice answer becomes a short video of up to 60 seconds with a
     waveform and captions. A poll becomes a results card with percentages.
     Link: every card carries a short link that opens the exact question or answer in the app,
     or on a fast mobile page if the app is not installed.
     Deep links: Android App Links and, later, iOS universal links. Use the Play Install
     Referrer to carry the link through installation so a new user lands on the same
     conversation.
     Preview tags: every public question and answer page has Open Graph and Twitter
     card tags so links preview well in WhatsApp and elsewhere.
     Attribution: every share carries the sharer's referral code, so signups from shares can
     be rewarded later.
     What is shared: a card and a link only. Raw audio files are never shared. This keeps the
     link back to the app and lets us remove content.
     Privacy: anonymous items share without an author. Confession sharing is off by
     default.
     Sharing is free, and every share records an intent event.

Rules

     Every placement is labelled and approved by a person before it runs, with evidence of
     any regulatory approval the law requires.
     No political advertising at launch. No betting, tobacco or health-claim adverts. Extra
     checks for alcohol and finance.
     Contextual targeting only, by question category, and by state or age band where users
     consented. No targeting on sensitive topics (politics, religion, health, sexual life).
     Frequency caps per user per day. Never inside confessions or beside sensitive
     conversations.
     Voice adverts play only when the user taps. No autoplay.
     Organic ranking and poll results are never influenced by advertisers.
     Launch with manual insertion orders. The admin dashboard stores campaigns,
     creative, dates, placements, caps and approval status.
     Pricing models in config: flat fee per day or week at launch, then cost per thousand
     views and cost per click later.
     Advertiser reports: views, taps, plays, listen-through, click-through, and spend.


6.10 Moderation and reporting
     Rules page, report on every answer, block and mute, spam prevention and rate limits.

     Voice clips are transcribed. All text and transcripts are screened for slurs, threats,
     scams and personal details such as phone numbers.
     Review by reach: any answer reaching 500 readers is checked by a moderator before it
     spreads further.
     A sensitive-topic queue (politics, religion, sex, tribe) gets extra review.
     Duplicate and coordinated voting detection, with an events mode and an election
     mode that tighten limits.
     Takedown process with a 24-hour target for agency notices, a priority route for people
     mentioned in posts, and an appeal route.
     All moderator actions are logged with a reason.


6.11 Notifications

10. Non-Functional Requirements
     Performance: feed loads in under 2 seconds on a typical Nigerian mobile connection.
     Voice playback starts within 2 seconds. Share card generation completes in under 5
     seconds.
     Low data: compressed audio, lazy loading, a data-saver mode, and light images.
     Devices: works well on low-end Android phones, with a small app size.
     Scale: designed for sudden spikes when a flash poll or question goes viral. Cache hot
     questions and poll results. Queue-based processing for transcription, share cards and
     notifications.
     Reliability: backups, monitoring, alerts, graceful failure, and a rollback plan.
     Security: encryption in transit and at rest, signed URLs, role-based access with audit
     logs, rate limits, and secure payment handling through the licensed partner. Card
     details never touch our servers.
     Accessibility: screen-reader labels, large text, high contrast, captions for voice.

     Consent screens at sign-up for data use and notifications. Political and religious

     App-store requirements: report, block, filter, and contact details inside the app, and

13. QA and Acceptance Criteria
 1. A guest can open a shared link, hear the answer and like it without signing up.
 2. A new user gets exactly 10 free posts. The 11th post shows the buy-units sheet and
     keeps the draft.
 3. Buying the Spark tier for ₦138 credits exactly 10 units. A duplicate payment callback
     does not credit twice.
 4. A 0.5-unit text post debits exactly 0.5 unit. A failed post reverses the debit.
 5. Buying Voice 30 debits 10.5 units, raises the recorder limit to 30 seconds, and the limit
     returns to 10 seconds when the pass ends.

 6. The balance never goes below zero, and the ledger totals match the payment partner's
     reconciliation.
 7. Voice recording stops automatically at the limit, and nothing publishes without Send.
 8. Reactions can be changed without being charged again, and likes and shares never
     charge.
 9. One vote per account, with results matching the vote log.
10. Sharing to WhatsApp and Instagram opens with a correct card and link, and the link
     opens the exact answer or installs the app and then opens it.
11. Sponsored items always show a label, never rank as organic, and never appear in the
     first 3 items or in sensitive areas.
12. An answer reaching 500 readers waits for moderator review before it spreads further.
13. An admin price change takes effect without an app update and is recorded in the
     change log.
14. Raw audio cannot be downloaded from the app or the web page.
