# LCH-14 · Design the launch screens

For the designer. The approved VOICES boards and design system are in the design pack (VOICES-design.zip).

## Boards to draw (phone 390 and desktop 1440, light and dark)
1. Mood pick on the launch question (four words, then record or type).
2. Answer cards and thread with the mood word beside each response.
3. "You’re live" redrawn: Share as the main button, WhatsApp, SMS, Copy link, share sheet, "Send it to 3 people.",
   "Ask the person next to you, then compare", the QR code.
4. The personal share card in 3 sizes (1200x630, 1080x1920, 1080x1080), with and without the name.
5. Where you sit: the Nigeria / World switch, the percentage, and "You’re one of the first."
6. Friends who answered: "2 of your friends have answered" and the list of words; the "Share my word" switch.
7. The voice video frame at 9:16 and 1:1 (waveform, captions, question, logo).
8. /go landing for the broadcast, with the new landing words (LCH-01).
9. Token packs and the daily limit message (if the new prices go in at launch), and Subscriptions (after launch).
10. "Fix my text" on Write.
11. The weekly Instagram carousel and Story.

## Rules
- The VOICES design system: Archivo and Bagel Fat One, the type scale, the tokens. The colour change (less dark green)
  is waiting on the owner’s yes.
- Money figures and rules come from PRICING.md, never drawn from memory.
- No em-dash in copy; never the word "wallet" for tokens; curly quotes.
